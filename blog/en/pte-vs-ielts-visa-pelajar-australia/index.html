PTE vs IELTS for the Australian student visa: 2026 minimum scores & which suits you

Updated: 21 November 2026

Share: WhatsApp Facebook LinkedIn X Telegram

Planning to study in Australia and unsure whether to take PTE Academic or IELTS? Both are accepted for the Australian student visa (subclass 500), but they differ in format, scoring scale and the type of test-taker they suit. This article summarises the official Home Affairs rules in force since 7 August 2025, the main differences between the two tests, and how to choose. We also run preparation classes in Pontianak and Singkawang through Victory Scholars Academy (VSA).

1. Minimum scores for the Australian student visa (tests from 7 August 2025)

Under the official Home Affairs instrument (LIN 25/090), which commenced on 7 August 2025, the minimum score for a subclass 500 visa depends on whether you take an English course (ELICOS) before your main course:

  • No ELICOS: IELTS Academic 6.0 / PTE Academic 47 / TOEFL iBT 67
  • With at least 10 weeks of ELICOS: IELTS 5.5 / PTE 39 / TOEFL iBT 51
  • With at least 20 weeks of ELICOS: IELTS 5.0 / PTE 31 / TOEFL iBT 37

For tests taken before 7 August 2025, the previous benchmarks apply: IELTS 6.0/5.5/5.0, PTE 50/42/36 and TOEFL iBT 64/46/35. The test must have been taken within 2 years before the visa application.

Important: these are visa minimums. Many schools and universities require higher scores for entry (for example Nursing or Teaching). You can check each programme's requirement in the Talented price list under programme details.

2. Key differences between PTE Academic and IELTS

  • Format: PTE Academic is fully computer-based in a single session of about 2 hours, including speaking recorded through a microphone. IELTS takes about 2 hours 45 minutes (Listening 30 min, Reading 60 min, Writing 60 min, Speaking 11-14 min), with a face-to-face speaking test.
  • Scoring scale: PTE uses a 10-90 scale; IELTS uses bands 0-9.
  • Result speed: Pearson says PTE results typically arrive within 48 hours (within 5 working days). IELTS on computer results are usually available within 1-2 days.
  • Sending scores: PTE scores can be sent to institutions an unlimited number of times at no extra cost.
  • Retaking one part: IELTS offers One Skill Retake, which Home Affairs accepts for visa purposes.

3. Do not use online/at-home tests for the visa

Home Affairs only accepts tests taken at a secure test centre. Online versions such as IELTS Online and TOEFL iBT Home Edition are not accepted for Australian visas. For TOEFL iBT, select "Taking TOEFL for Australia" when registering.

4. Which one suits you?

No test is "easier" for everyone. As a general guide:

  • PTE often suits people who are comfortable typing on a computer, want fast results, and feel calmer speaking to a microphone than to an examiner.
  • IELTS often suits people who prefer speaking with a real person, are used to writing longer essays, or whose target school is more familiar with IELTS.

The most objective way: try the Free PTE & IELTS Mock Test on the Talented website, then compare your results and comfort in both formats. Also make sure your target school accepts the test you choose.

Quick FAQ

What PTE score do I need for an Australian student visa without ELICOS?
At least 47 (tests from 7 August 2025). School requirements may be higher.

Can I still use a PTE 50 from an older test?
Tests taken before 7 August 2025 use the previous benchmarks (50/42/36), provided the test was taken within 24 months before the visa application.

Is IELTS Online accepted for Australian visas?
No. Use a test taken at an official test centre.

Is PTE Core the same as PTE Academic?
No. For Australian visas, use PTE Academic.
