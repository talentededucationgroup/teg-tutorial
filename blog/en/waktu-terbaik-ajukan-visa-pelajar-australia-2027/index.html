When is the best time to lodge an Australian student visa for 2027 study?

Updated: 21 November 2026

Share: WhatsApp Facebook LinkedIn X Telegram

Students often ask: when is the best time to lodge an Australian student visa (subclass 500)? The answer is not only "how many weeks before classes" but also the processing priority fixed on the day you lodge. Here is a summary of the official rules in force and Talented's timing advice.

1. Current rules: Ministerial Direction 115 In force

For applications lodged from 14 November 2025, Home Affairs processes student visas in 3 priority levels:

  • Priority 1 - including school, non-award and PhD students, government scholarship holders, and higher education/VET students whose provider has not yet reached 80% of its new-student allocation. Target: 1-4 weeks from lodgement.
  • Priority 2 - the provider has reached 80% of its allocation. Target: 5-8 weeks.
  • Priority 3 - the provider has exceeded its allocation by more than 15%. Target: 9-12 weeks.

Priority is locked on the day you lodge and does not change. It only affects speed, not whether the visa is granted or refused.

2. Why November-December is the golden window

Applications lodged between 15 November and 31 December count against the following year's allocation. Right now (late 2026), applications for 2027 study count against the fresh 2027 allocation, so most providers are still far below the 80% threshold.

Talented's estimate (not a guarantee): the earlier in the allocation year you lodge, the better your chance of Priority 1. Popular providers usually move closer to the 80% threshold ahead of mid-year intakes.

3. Suggested lodgement timing for 2027 intakes

  • February-March 2027 intake: secure your offer letter, pay the initial payment and get your CoE as soon as possible; lodge November 2026 - early January 2027.
  • July 2027 intake: aim to have your CoE and lodge February - April 2027; don't wait until 4-6 weeks before classes.
  • October-November 2027 intake: lodge at least 3 months before classes and check the provider's allocation status first.

4. Check your provider's status before lodging

University priority status is published by the Department of Education (Visa Prioritisation Status). For VET providers, you must ask the provider directly - a Talented consultant can ask for you. For packaged courses (e.g. ELICOS + Diploma), priority follows the main CoE, usually the final course.

5. What still decides the outcome

  • Genuine Student: a clear, sensible study reason.
  • Financial capacity: prepare bank statements using the simulation on the Talented price list.
  • English requirements and OSHC for the whole visa period.
  • Since 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders must apply for a student visa from outside Australia (exceptions apply).

Talented monitors official rule changes and data daily. This article will be updated when anything changes.
