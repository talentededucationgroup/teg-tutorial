Student visa update November 2026: 6 key changes in Australia, NZ, UK, Canada & the US

Updated: 18 November 2026

WhatsApp Facebook LinkedIn X Telegram

This month brings several official changes (and one public consultation) that matter for students planning to study overseas in 2027. Every point below comes from government websites - see Sources.

1. Australia: Genuine Student & financial capacity consultation Public consultation

Home Affairs is consulting (closes 23 November 2026) on updating Ministerial Direction 106 for the Genuine Student assessment, strengthening financial capacity requirements, and a possible interim measure for Evidence Level 3 applicants. No decisions have been made - current rules still apply.

What it means for you: prepare clear financial documents and a well-explained study plan early. Talented's price list already estimates the minimum bank statement for every programme.

2. New Zealand: Short-Term Graduate Work Visa (6 months) In force

From 16 November 2026, graduates of NZQCF Level 5-7 qualifications (at least 24 weeks full-time in NZ) who are not eligible for a Post-Study Work Visa can get 6 months of open work rights. Apply within 3 months of your student visa expiring and show NZD 5,000 in living funds.

3. New Zealand: Post-Study Work Visa eligibility extended In force

Also from 16 November 2026: Level 7 Graduate Diploma graduates who already hold a bachelor's degree (NZ or overseas) can get a PSWV of up to 1 year. Postgraduate Diploma (Level 8) up to 1 year and Master's (Level 9) up to 3 years, with at least 30 weeks of full-time study in NZ.

4. UK: maintenance funds rise on 30 November 2026 Upcoming

For Student visa applications from 30 November 2026: London GBP 1,570/month (max. 9 months = GBP 14,130) and outside London GBP 1,203/month (max. GBP 10,827). Funds must be held for 28 consecutive days.

5. Canada: 150,000 new students per year (2027-2029) Announced

The 2027-2029 Immigration Levels Plan sets 150,000 new international student arrivals per year (2026: 155,000). Postgraduate study remains a priority.

6. United States: court vacates the rule replacing D/S Changed

On 16 November 2026 a Massachusetts federal court vacated the DHS rule that replaced Duration of Status (D/S) with fixed admission periods. D/S remains for now; an appeal is expected.
