2. Why "unlimited"?

There is no attempt limit. Questions are shuffled each time, so you can retake the same test many times to build speed and score consistency. No fee, no credit card and no sign-up.

3. Key features

  • Exam-style timer to get used to time pressure.

4. How to use mock tests to raise your score

  1. Take one full test first to set your baseline.
  2. Note your weakest section and drill that section several times.
  3. Retake a full test every 1–2 weeks and compare scores.
  4. Book the official exam once your practice score is consistently above your target.
